This notice is for stratton.life, the family and ministry site of Nathan and Azusa Stratton. We do not run ads, and we do not use Google Analytics or other tracking services.

## The contact form

If you write to us through the form on the Reach Out page, you give us your name, your email address, and whatever you put in the message. We use that only to read your note and reply. We do not sell it, and we do not add you to a mailing list unless you ask us to.

The form is delivered by [Formspree](https://formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy/). Formspree receives the message in order to pass it to us. Their privacy policy explains what they do with it on their side.

## Mail

If you write us by post, we use that address only to correspond with you.

## Videos and links

Some posts include a video played from X. Opening that page loads the player from X, under [X’s privacy policy](https://x.com/en/privacy).

Some posts include a video played from Vimeo. Opening that page loads the player from Vimeo, under [Vimeo’s privacy policy](https://vimeo.com/privacy). Vimeo receives technical information when the player loads, such as the page address and the browser. We use Vimeo only to store those videos and play them here. They are not posted as public videos on Vimeo, and the player is limited to sites run by the Strattons. We do not use the Vimeo Like or Share buttons.

The X icon in the header is an ordinary link. It does not load X until you click it.

## Asking us to delete something

Use the contact form and say what you want removed. We will delete the message and our reply when we are reasonably able to. Pages and photos we published ourselves stay up until we choose to change them.

## Children

Please do not send us personal information about a child through the form unless you are the parent and you need to for a specific reason.
